# Brief: quiet text

A page whose only content is text, read for twenty minutes without fatigue. No imagery is provided and none should be invented.

## Domains

- **long-form reading page**: an essay of about 2500 words with a title, a byline, five section headings, two block quotes and four footnotes.
- **policy document viewer**: a 3000-word policy with numbered clauses to three levels, a definitions list, an effective date, and a change log of four dated entries.

## Constraints

- Self-hosted or system fonts only; state which.
- The measure stays between 55 and 80 characters at every width.
- Footnotes and clause references must be reachable without losing the reading position.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Headings, body, quotes and notes are distinguishable by type alone at 390 px and 1440 px.
2. The measure criterion holds at 390, 768 and 1440 px.
3. A footnote or clause reference can be followed and returned from in two interactions or fewer.
4. The policy's clause hierarchy is legible without reading the numbers.
5. The two domains read differently (an essay invites, a policy is consulted) through rhythm, density or hierarchy, not through decoration.
