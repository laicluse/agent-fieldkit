# Brief: marketing

One page that introduces a product to someone who has never heard of it and gets them to take one action.

## Domains

- **developer tool**: a CLI that keeps a coding agent out of a directory another agent is working in. Content: the name `dibs`, one paragraph on what it does, the install command, three facts (cross-platform lock, takes over stale locks, works with two agent vendors), a link to the source.
- **neighbourhood bakery**: a sourdough bakery open three mornings a week. Content: the name, the street, the three days and hours, the four breads with prices, one sentence about the starter's age, an order link.

## Constraints

- No stock imagery; the only images are ones the brief provides or ones the agent renders from the product itself.
- The page must work at 390 px and 1440 px.
- No more than one primary action.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The single action is visible within the first viewport at both widths.
2. Every content item in the domain's list appears on the page.
3. Text contrast meets WCAG AA for body and large text.
4. Nothing overflows horizontally at 390 px.
5. The two domains do not share a layout: a viewer shown both pages with names removed can say which is the bakery.
