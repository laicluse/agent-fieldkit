# Method and evidence

This workflow is a practical synthesis, not proof that one model family has universally better taste. Evaluate it by accepted real outcomes and the cost of reaching them.

- [OpenAI: How to build games with Astra](https://developers.openai.com/blog/how-to-build-games-with-astra): a development case combining visual assets and implementation. A showcase demonstrates feasibility, not typical success rates or a controlled image-model versus coding-model comparison.
- [StyleAligned](https://style-aligned-gen.github.io/): research on consistent image sets using shared attention. It supports the value of consistency mechanisms, but ordinary reference prompting does not reproduce that exact method.
- [Anthropic: Harness design for long-running apps](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps): practitioner evidence for iteration and evaluation. Independent review needs concrete calibration and is not an aesthetic oracle.

The transferable workflow is to anchor exploration in images, elicit reactions to visible proposals, preserve accepted decisions and inspect the real implementation. Image generation remains fallible: a beautiful still can hide impossible geometry, incorrect states or an interaction that fails in motion.
