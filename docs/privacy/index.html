l'Aicluse privacy

Updated 27 October 2026

Purpose and data

We process information you provide and data from accounts you connect, such as account details and purchase records, only for the features you choose.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored locally or processed by services used for the selected feature, under their privacy policies. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Your control

You can revoke account access in the connected service's settings. Local copies remain until deleted. For questions or data requests, contact l'Aicluse.
